Aegean Fish Simulator

Native and invasive Aegean fish find food, grow, breed and die while bluefish and lionfish hunt them. Each species eats different food, keeps to its own part of the water and copes with a different range of sea temperatures. Babies inherit their parent's speed and eyesight with small random changes, so the populations evolve.

Click or tap the water to drop feed pellets — every species eats them, as wild fish do around fish farms.
